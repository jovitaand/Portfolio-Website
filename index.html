I’m a Master’s graduate in Applied Data Science from Syracuse University, with a foundation in Computer Science and Engineering. My work brings together statistical analysis, AI systems, and reproducible research.

At NYU Abu Dhabi, I analyze multi-omics data to investigate how chronic stress affects adipose tissue biology. Previously, at Syracuse’s iSchool, I developed conversational AI systems using retrieval-augmented generation, vector search, and large language models.

I’m interested in careful questions, responsible AI, and research that connects computational methods with problems that matter.
