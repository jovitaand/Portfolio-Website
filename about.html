BEHIND THE WORK

A curious mind.
A global perspective.

Hey there, I’m Jovita.

Born in India and raised in the UAE, I bring a global perspective to my work. My journey took me to Syracuse University in New York for a Master’s in Applied Data Science. I’m now based in Abu Dhabi.

I love diving deep into machine learning, natural language processing, and predictive models, and I’m always looking for ways to make data more impactful and ethical.

Today, I work as a Biostatistician at NYU Abu Dhabi’s Center for Brain and Health, building reproducible Python and R workflows for multi-omics research on chronic stress. Previously, I developed conversational AI systems, RAG pipelines, and agent-assistance tools at Syracuse’s iSchool.

When I’m not immersed in code or cloud platforms, you can find me swimming, catching up with friends, or adding new projects to GitHub. Here’s to making waves and meaningful insights, one dataset at a time.
